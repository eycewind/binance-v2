## 1. Objective

C6 established on its frozen 439-trade prediction-complete population that exiting at the first causal T2 state (`S1`) strongly outperformed the immutable original exit (`P0`), while G1 was significantly worse than S1 and the formal hierarchy stopped at Gate 2. C7 asks whether that simple, model-free exit effect extends to the complete eligible original-entry development population rather than only to trades having complete G1/P1 prediction paths.

C7 is an **exit overlay** on frozen original entries. It is not a new entry strategy, a portfolio backtest, or a model experiment. Entry generation, entry execution, trade size, opportunity set, and the original exit remain unchanged. An earlier overlay exit never creates a replacement entry, re-entry, extra position, capital recycling, or compounding.

## 2. Scientific hypothesis and single primary question

The single primary question is:

> Does exiting at the immediate next one-minute open after the first causally observable T2 state improve the original strategy, net of realistic `C_BASE` costs, on the maximum complete eligible 2024–2025 original-entry development cohort?

The confirmatory contrast is:

```text
Delta_PRIMARY = mean net return(S1_FULL_T2_OVERLAY, C_BASE)
                - mean net return(P0_FULL, C_BASE)
```

The directional hypothesis is `Delta_PRIMARY > 0`; the null is `Delta_PRIMARY <= 0`. A positive point estimate alone is not support. Section 17 freezes the complete interpretation rule.

## 6. Exact T2 definition by frozen reference

C7 reuses, without alteration, C4 target ID `C4_T2_GIVEBACK50_VS_NEWPEAK050_H60_V1` **only for its causal landmark eligibility state**, as carried through C5/C6. At every eligible completed UTC-aligned five-minute snapshot `t`:

- the latest included source minute is `[t-1m,t)` and the snapshot reference price `C_t` is its close;
- `t` is strictly after entry execution and strictly before the immutable P0 horizon/execution point;
- the completed five-minute aggregate is wholly post-entry;
- with frozen entry price `E`, `P_t` is the maximum valid post-entry high observable by `t`, including the completed bar ending at `t`;
- `G_t = P_t / E - 1` and `R_t = C_t / E - 1`;
- eligibility requires `G_t >= 0.0100`, `R_t > 0`, and `C_t > D2`, where `D2 = E * (1 + 0.50 * G_t) = (E + P_t) / 2`; and
- the frozen renewed-peak barrier is `U2 = P_t * 1.0050`, although neither the future T2 event class nor its 60-minute outcome is needed for this overlay.

`P_t`, `G_t`, `R_t`, and `D2` use only information available at `t`. `C_t == D2` is ineligible. A state at or below `D2` is `already_beyond_giveback_state`, not T2. No future high may redefine the snapshot. The **first causal T2** is the earliest canonically ordered snapshot satisfying all eligibility conditions. C7 must reproduce accepted frozen T2 row identities wherever its full-population rows overlap an accepted upstream raw-T2 table; mismatch fails T1.

## 8. Execution timing, precedence, ties, and gaps

A T2 decision at completed boundary `t` executes at the raw open of `[t,t+1m)`. This interval was not contained in the decision snapshot. The fill may not use the snapshot close, a barrier price, a later substitute bar, or a favorable intra-minute price.

Original-exit precedence is frozen as follows:

1. Only an overlay execution timestamp **strictly earlier** than the canonical P0 execution timestamp may replace P0.
2. If the T2 decision timestamp equals the P0 decision/execution boundary, if the immediate-next-minute overlay execution timestamp equals P0 execution, or if it is later, P0 wins and the trade is classified `P0_PRECEDENCE_TIE_OR_NOT_EARLIER`.
3. A completed T2 snapshot must itself be strictly before P0; no state completed at P0 is actionable.
4. If P0 is already due at the next open following a T2 decision, the equal execution timestamp is a tie and P0 wins.

Thus there is no ambiguous simultaneous fill and no candidate advantage from tie ordering. If the immediate next-minute open needed for a potentially earlier overlay is missing, invalid, nonfinite, or nonpositive, do not jump the gap and do not silently fall back to P0: paired resolution is unavailable and the trade is excluded under its frozen T1 reason. A pre-snapshot gap that breaks causal peak or aggregate construction likewise prevents classification across the gap. No forward fill, nearest value, interpolation, cross-symbol borrowing, or gap-skipping is permitted.

## 10. Primary estimand and pairing

The experimental unit is one whole frozen original entry. On the immutable included population, both policies must have exactly one row for each `trade_id`; no unmatched analysis is allowed.

For trade `i`, define:

```text

      - net_return_i(P0_FULL, C_BASE)
Delta_PRIMARY = arithmetic_mean_i(d_i)
```

This trade-weighted paired mean is primary. 3. form every non-circular block of four consecutive week positions;
4. sample blocks with replacement until the observed span length is reached, truncate excess positions, and preserve integer trade multiplicity without deduplication; and

## 14. Mandatory adverse-safety checks

- green-to-red incidence is higher for S1 (interval entirely above zero);
- preserved-at-least-half-of-common-peak incidence is lower for S1 (interval entirely below zero); or
- within any immutable P0 raw-return winner cohort `+2%`, `+3%`, or `+5%` whose safety interval is available under the rule below, mean net-return difference is lower for S1 (interval entirely below zero).

C5/C6 freeze the interval direction and the general block-bootstrap feasibility conditions but do not state an exact support rule applicable to these pooled winner-cohort mean-difference intervals. C7 therefore prospectively applies this identical statistical-feasibility rule to each of the `+2%`, `+3%`, and `+5%` cohorts. A cohort safety interval is **available** if and only if:

1. the cohort contains at least **50 distinct included trades**;
2. the Section 13 primary entry-week span contains at least four week positions and at least two nonempty candidate four-week blocks for that cohort; and
3. every one of the frozen 2,000 primary bootstrap replicates selects at least one cohort trade and produces a finite paired cohort mean net-return difference.

Otherwise that cohort interval and its mandatory safety classification are **unavailable**, the exact failed condition is reported, and the overall C7 label is `INSUFFICIENT` under Section 17. The common 50-trade minimum is adopted solely to avoid formal tail-safety inference from a small conditioned cohort; it is identical across the three thresholds and is not based on C7 economics.
