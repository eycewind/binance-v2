# Exploratory interpretation

This diagnostic characterizes frozen C5 behavior only. G1 triggered at its first T2 snapshot for 249 of 439 trades; P1 did so for 227 of 439. A slim majority was immediate, while the near-universal total was completed by later first passages that were usually early in the eligible path. The result is therefore rapid eventual triggering, not “almost everyone immediately.” Path-length context is reported separately and no economic calculation was made.

No result here establishes that exiting at T2 entry, after a delay, or randomly has economic value. No returns, P&L, costs, future outcomes, MFE, P0 outcomes, or winner truncation were used.
